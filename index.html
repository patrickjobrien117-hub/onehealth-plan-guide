OneHealth Plus Plan Overview

Use this page as a quick-reference study tool. Review important plan concepts, common terminology, workflow steps, FAQs, and quiz questions.

Quick Reference

Plan Administration

OneHealth Plus helps coordinate employer health-plan administration, member support, plan education, and related program services.

Network Access

Members may access provider networks according to the specific health plan selected by the employer.

Member Support

Members may need help understanding eligibility, benefits, claims, provider access, and plan navigation.

Employer Support

Employers may receive assistance with implementation, plan communication, enrollment support, and ongoing administration.

Key Terms

Deductible

The amount a member may need to pay for covered services before the plan begins paying certain benefits.

Copay

A fixed amount a member may pay for a covered service, such as a doctor visit.

Coinsurance

The percentage of covered healthcare costs a member may pay after meeting the applicable deductible.

Out-of-Pocket Maximum

The maximum amount a member generally pays for covered in-network services during a plan year before the plan pays 100% of covered eligible costs.

In-Network

Providers or facilities that participate in the plan's contracted network.

Out-of-Network

Providers or facilities that do not participate in the contracted plan network.

Eligibility

The rules that determine whether an employee or dependent qualifies for coverage.

Effective Date

The date coverage officially begins.

Typical Workflow

Step 1

Receive Request

Identify whether the request involves enrollment, eligibility, benefits, claims, implementation, or member support.

Step 2

Verify Information

Confirm employer group, member information, plan selection, effective date, and relevant documentation.

Step 3

Research

Review plan materials, internal systems, carrier resources, and applicable procedures.

Step 4

Resolve or Escalate

Complete the request when possible or escalate to the appropriate internal team or external partner.

Step 5

Document

Record actions taken, communication, status, and any required follow-up.

Step 6

Follow Up

Confirm resolution and make sure the employer, member, or internal stakeholder receives the needed update.

Frequently Asked Questions

Verify the member, employer group, coverage status, effective date, plan information, and the exact issue being asked about.
Avoid guessing. Research the correct plan documents or system information, then escalate when necessary.
Documentation creates a clear history of the request, actions taken, communications, decisions, and follow-up.
Protected health information and other sensitive member information should only be handled according to company privacy and security procedures.
